# N. A title that states the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

Revised: YYYY-MM-DD, only after a revision in place. Revise in place only
to correct a fact, or to move text into another ADR with a pointer left
behind. A new or reversed decision is a new ADR, which names the ADR or
the part of one it supersedes.

An ADR stands on its own. It cites other ADRs, and outside sources such as
specifications and library docs, but no other document of this project: no
roadmap, guides, CHANGELOG, fixture walks, phases, or plans. What it needs
from such a document, it states in its own words.

## Status

Accepted, or Superseded by [ADR N](000N-title.md)

## Context

The forces, stated as facts: what exists, what must hold, and what is being
decided. No verdicts on options; those go in Rationale.

## Decision

What PureHTML does, stated in full sentences.

## Rationale

Why this option, with its evidence: what was measured or reproduced, on
what data or versions, and how often.

**A rejected option.** One paragraph each, led by the option in bold: what
it would have done and the fact that ruled it out.

## Consequences

- What follows, good and bad: costs taken on, and rules later code must
  keep.
- What is untested, and what would reopen the decision.
